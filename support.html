Shift Pocket Calendar Support

Shift Pocket Calendar helps you keep shift templates, reminders, and estimated pay in one lightweight iPhone workflow.

Getting Started

  • Create one or more shift templates in Settings or Templates.
  • Open Calendar or Home to add shifts for the days you need.
  • Use Quick Schedule or Weekly Pattern to fill repeated schedules faster.

Troubleshooting

  • No reminder arrived: check notification permission for Shift Pocket Calendar in iPhone Settings.
  • Calendar export failed: make sure calendar access is granted when you tap Export.
  • Hours or pay look wrong: review start time, end time, break length, and hourly rate in the shift entry.

Data Notes

The current version stores scheduling data locally on device and does not require an account login for the main workflow.
